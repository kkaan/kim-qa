Section 01 · The Challenge

The prostate moves during treatment

During radiation therapy, the prostate can drift, shift, and rotate due to bladder filling, rectal gas, and patient breathing. In hypofractionated SBRT, where each fraction delivers a high dose over fewer sessions, even millimetre-scale motion can compromise treatment accuracy and increase toxicity to surrounding healthy tissue.

Traditional image guidance captures a snapshot before treatment begins, but the target can move significantly during the minutes of beam delivery. Without real-time monitoring, these intra-fraction motions go undetected and uncorrected.

We need to see the target while the beam is on.
Section 02 · The Technology

Kilovoltage Intrafraction Monitoring

KIM is a real-time image guidance system developed at the Image X Institute, University of Sydney. It uses the existing kV imaging hardware on standard linear accelerators to track implanted fiducial markers in real time, with no additional equipment required.

STEP 01

Acquire

The gantry-mounted kV imager continuously captures 2D projection images of implanted gold fiducial markers during beam delivery at 5 to 10 frames per second.

STEP 02

Reconstruct

KIM software segments the markers in each frame and reconstructs their 3D positions in six degrees of freedom, three translations and three rotations, using a probability-based matching algorithm.

STEP 03

Adapt

If motion exceeds protocol thresholds (3 mm or more for 5 s or more in PRIME), KIM signals the radiation therapists to pause the beam. A couch correction returns the target to the planned position before treatment resumes.

Section 03 · The PRIME Trial

Real-time KIM guidance for prostate radiotherapy

PRIME is a GenesisCare-sponsored, prospective, single-arm feasibility study assessing real-time kilovoltage intrafraction monitoring and manual gating for prostate radiotherapy in men with prostate adenocarcinoma. Conducted across 10 Australian centres with support from the Image X Institute at the University of Sydney, PRIME brings sub-millimetre intra-fraction tracking from research into routine clinical practice.

Objective

Assess the feasibility of KIM real-time monitoring with manual gating for prostate radiotherapy delivery across multiple centres, evaluating whether the technology can be reliably integrated into routine clinical workflows.

Intervention

Patients receive standard-of-care external beam radiotherapy with continuous KIM monitoring at 5 to 10 Hz during beam delivery. KIM tracks three intraprostatic fiducial markers in real time. If prostate motion of 3 mm or more persists for 5 seconds or more, KIM signals the radiation therapists to pause the beam and apply couch corrections before resuming.

Primary endpoints

Proportion of fractions successfully delivered with KIM as per protocol (no KIM-related technical failure), and percentage of fractions where total couch time is completed within 25 minutes.

AI sub-study

Technical data from the PRIME cohort, kV frames, position logs, plan and structure sets, will be used to train and validate AI-based marker and markerless tracking models developed by Image X Institute.

Section 04 · QA Tools

Open-source quality assurance

Each PRIME centre undergoes commissioning and ongoing QA with the tools in this repository. The KIM QA Analysis app compares KIM's real-time tracking against a 6 DoF robotic phantom playing programmed motion traces, and reports the tracking error that decides pass or fail.

An interrupted prostate acquisition: KIM detections on the HexaMotion input trace, dead time compressed, couch correction annotated per axis.

Unified overlay analysis

Point the app at a results folder and it discovers every session: static localisation, dynamic motion traces, and interrupted treatments with couch corrections. Auto-aligned overlays, live tracking-error metrics (mean, SD, 5th and 95th percentiles per axis), kV frame preview on hover, and a combined QA summary report.

Section 05 · Download & Use

Run the QA app in four steps

STEP 01 · DOWNLOAD

Get the latest release

Download KIM-QA-Analysis-Windows.zip from the latest release and extract it anywhere. No installer and no Python required; everything ships inside KIM-QA-Server.exe.

STEP 02 · LAUNCH

Point it at your results

Double-click KIM-QA-Server.exe and pick your results folder in the dialog, or run KIM-QA-Server.exe --root "D:\KIM\2026-06-23" from a terminal. For Varian machines add --vendor Varian. Your browser opens the app automatically.

STEP 03 · ANALYSE

Review each session

Every session folder appears in the sidebar, classified as static, motion, or interrupted. Motion traces auto-match by folder name; fine-tune the time alignment with the offset slider, drag on a panel to select analysis ranges, and hover points to see the kV frame behind each detection.

STEP 04 · SIGN OFF

Save the QA record

Save writes overlay.png into the session folder and regenerates a combined summary.md at the root with per-axis and 3D tracking-error statistics for every reviewed session.
